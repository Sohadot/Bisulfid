Sulfur Compounds

Sulfur forms a rich family of inorganic compound classes. This atlas covers the sulfide and bisulfide branches of that family, with disambiguation coverage of adjacent classes (sulfite, sulfate). Each compound class has a German and an English name that differ by the -id/-ide suffix.

The Core Bisulfide/Sulfide Family

The central compound classes of this atlas are:

  • Bisulfide / Bisulfid — HS¹¯ compounds; the domain identity term class
  • Sulfide / Sulfid — S²¯ compounds
  • Hydrosulfide — alternative name for HS¹¯ compounds
  • Disulfide / Disulfid — S–S bond compounds
  • Polysulfide / Polysulfid — S⊂n²¯ chain compounds

Parent Compound

Hydrogen sulfide (H&sub2;S) is the parent compound of the bisulfide and sulfide families. Losing one proton from H&sub2;S gives HS¹¯ (bisulfide/hydrosulfide ion). Losing both protons gives S²¯ (sulfide ion).

Disambiguation: Outside the Sulfide Family

These compound classes are adjacent to, but distinct from, the sulfide family:

  • Bisulfite (HSO&sub3;¹¯) — sulfite compound; not bisulfide
  • Sulfite (SO&sub3;²¯) — sulfur-oxygen compound; not sulfide
  • Sulfat (SO&sub4;²¯) — sulfate; fully oxidized; not sulfide
