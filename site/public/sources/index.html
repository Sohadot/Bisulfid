Source Discipline

bisulfid.com operates under a strict source discipline principle: no claim reaches a public page without a corresponding verified source. This page explains the framework, the accepted source categories, and the permanent restrictions on what this site may publish.

The Governing Principle

Every factual claim that appears on a public-facing page of bisulfid.com must be supported by a traceable, credentialled source. If no verified source exists for a claim, that claim does not appear on the public surface.

This means the public site may contain less content than readers expect at any given time. That is intentional. Accuracy and source integrity take precedence over completeness. The atlas grows only as fast as its sources can be verified.

What Source Discipline Means for Readers

When you read a claim on bisulfid.com, it has been held against the source discipline framework before appearing here. When a claim cannot pass that framework, it remains unpublished.

bisulfid.com does not present speculative claims as established fact. It does not use rhetorical framing to imply authority it has not earned through source verification. Where framing is cautious rather than definitive, that is intentional — not evasion, but precision.

Source Categories

The source framework is designed to accept the following source types:

  • Chemical nomenclature authorities — recognised bodies that govern compound naming conventions, including IUPAC and DIN standards.
  • Scientific literature — peer-reviewed publications relevant to terminology and nomenclature claims.
  • Industrial reference databases — verified industry data relevant to sulfur compounds and their documented applications.
  • Regulatory bodies — official sources where regulatory context is relevant to the claim.

What Is Permanently Blocked

The following are permanently outside the scope of bisulfid.com, regardless of source availability:

  • Safety thresholds, handling instructions, or exposure guidance of any kind
  • Medical or therapeutic claims
  • Bisulfite data presented as bisulfide data — the two compounds are chemically distinct
  • AI-generated summaries presented as primary sources
  • Unsourced numerical market claims or unattributed statistics

Role in the Bisulfid Atlas

This page is the governance anchor of the bisulfid.com reference surface. The source discipline framework applies to every other page in the atlas. The Glossary terms, the What is Bisulfid? definitions, the spelling boundary explanation, and the language layer descriptions are all subject to this framework.

Pages on bisulfid.com carry individual source-posture notices at their base precisely because no claim is approved by default.
