What the Asset Is

bisulfid.com uses the spelling Bisulfid. Whether it is simply the German form of the English word bisulfide is not established by the sources examined so far. In English, the Merriam-Webster dictionary defines bisulfide as disulfide, while the PubChem database lists bisulfide as a synonym for the hydrosulfide ion (HS−). In German, the DWDS (Digitales Wörterbuch der deutschen Sprache) reports that Bisulfid is not among its contemporary lexical sources; this describes that one dictionary, not every German dictionary or technical text.

In German inorganic nomenclature the name of the negatively charged partner of a binary compound ends in -id where English uses -ide, as in Sulfid and sulfide; a German university teaching text on nomenclature states this rule. The domain name uses that spelling pattern. The pattern explains the form of the name, but it does not by itself establish Bisulfid as the standardized German equivalent of bisulfide.

A sourced terminology reference on these questions is being prepared. It has not been published yet, and this page does not depend on its conclusions.
