The Core Sulfide Family

At the center of this atlas are three closely related compound classes built around the hydrogen sulfide chemistry:

Bisulfide / Bisulfid

The bisulfide class contains compounds of the HS¹¯ ion — the hydrogen sulfide anion. This ion is formed when hydrogen sulfide (H&sub2;S) loses one proton. Sodium bisulfide (NaHS) is a typical example. The German name Bisulfid follows the -id convention; the English name bisulfide uses -ide. This term is the domain identity term for bisulfid.com.

Sulfide / Sulfid

The sulfide class contains compounds of the S²¯ ion — the sulfide dianion. This ion carries a charge of −2. Sodium sulfide (Na&sub2;S) and iron(II) sulfide (FeS) are examples. The German name is Sulfid; the English name is sulfide.

Hydrosulfide

Hydrosulfide is an alternative name for bisulfide — both refer to HS¹¯ compounds. The two names are used in different contexts; hydrosulfide is sometimes preferred in academic chemistry, while bisulfide is more common in industrial and commercial usage. German: Hydrosulfid.

Extended Sulfide Classes

Disulfide / Disulfid

Disulfide compounds contain a sulfur–sulfur (S–S) single bond. The disulfide bond appears both in inorganic chemistry (persulfide anion S&sub2;²¯) and in organic chemistry (R–S–S–R), where it plays an important structural role in proteins and industrial polymers. Molybdenum disulfide (MoS&sub2;) contains two sulfide atoms per formula unit, not a disulfide bond per se. German: Disulfid.

Polysulfide

Polysulfide compounds contain chains of sulfur atoms as the anion: S⊂n²¯ where n is 3 or more. These are found in nature (polysulfide minerals) and produced industrially. German: Polysulfid.

Thiols: Organosulfur Neighbors

Thiols (R–SH) are organic compounds containing a sulfur–hydrogen bond. They are sometimes called mercaptans. Thiols are adjacent to the bisulfide chemistry — the HS¹¯ anion is the deprotonated form of H&sub2;S, and thiols are organic analogs with a carbon-substituted sulfur — but they are a distinct compound class. bisulfid.com covers thiols only as a boundary node.

Compounds Outside the Sulfide Family

The following compound classes are covered in this atlas only as disambiguation nodes. They are distinct from sulfides and bisulfides:

  • Bisulfite (HSO&sub3;¹¯) — a sulfite compound; no relationship to bisulfide
  • Sulfite (SO&sub3;²¯) — a sulfur-oxygen compound; not a sulfide
  • Thiosulfate (S&sub2;O&sub3;²¯) — contains both sulfur and oxygen; distinct from sulfide
  • Sulfuric acid (H&sub2;SO&sub4;) — an acid, not a sulfide compound
