Galena (PbS)

Galena is the lead sulfide mineral with the formula PbS. It belongs to the sulfide compound class — one lead(II) ion (Pb²+) per sulfide ion (S²¯). German: Galenit. Galena is historically the most important lead ore mineral.

Composition and Structure

Galena has the formula PbS: one lead and one sulfur in a 1:1 ratio. The lead ion carries a +2 charge, balanced by the sulfide dianion S²¯. Galena crystallizes in the rock salt structure (same as NaCl), a cubic arrangement where each lead ion is surrounded by six sulfide ions and vice versa. It has a distinctive bright metallic lead-gray luster.

Galena in the Sulfide Mineral Family

Galena is a classic member of the sulfide mineral family alongside:

  • Sphalerite (ZnS) — zinc sulfide mineral
  • Pyrrhotite (FeS) — iron sulfide mineral
  • Pyrite (FeS&sub2;) — iron disulfide mineral

All of these are sulfide (or disulfide) minerals, and all are covered in the Bisulfid Atlas as reference examples of the compound classes.

German: Galenit

In German, galena is Galenit (mineral name) or Bleisulfid (lead sulfide as a compound name). Bleisulfid follows the standard German naming: Blei (lead) + Sulfid (the -id form of sulfide).
