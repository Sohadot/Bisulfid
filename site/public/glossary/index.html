The DE/EN Suffix Pattern

German chemical nomenclature systematically uses the suffix -id where English uses -ide. This applies across the sulfide compound family and to many other compound classes:

  • English bisulfide → German Bisulfid
  • English sulfide → German Sulfid
  • English oxide → German Oxid
  • English chloride → German Chlorid

bisulfid.com maps this pattern as part of its multilingual nomenclature architecture. The full explanation is at Bisulfid vs Bisulfide. The language layer overview is at Language Boundary.
