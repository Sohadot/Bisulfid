Ion Reference · Sulfide Class · S²⁻

The Sulfide Ion (S²⁻)

The sulfide ion (S²¯) is the sulfur dianion — a sulfur atom carrying a charge of −2. It is the defining ion of the sulfide compound class (German: Sulfid). The sulfide ion is related to, but distinct from, the bisulfide ion (HS¹¯) which carries a single negative charge.

Formation

The sulfide dianion S²¯ is formed by the complete deprotonation of hydrogen sulfide (H&sub2;S). This is a two-step process:

H&sub2;S → HS¹¯ + H¹+ (first step: gives the bisulfide ion)
HS¹¯ → S²¯ + H¹+ (second step: gives the sulfide dianion)

The sulfide ion therefore represents the fully deprotonated form of hydrogen sulfide.

Sulfide vs Bisulfide: The Ion Difference

The two ions are distinct species with different charges and behaviors:

  • S²¯ (sulfide ion, charge −2) → forms compounds like Na&sub2;S, FeS, ZnS. These are the sulfide / Sulfid class.
  • HS¹¯ (bisulfide/hydrosulfide ion, charge −1) → forms compounds like NaHS. These are the bisulfide / Bisulfid class.

Common Sulfide Ion Compounds

  • Sodium sulfide (Na&sub2;S) — two sodium ions per sulfide ion
  • Iron(II) sulfide (FeS) — one iron ion per sulfide ion
  • Zinc sulfide (ZnS) — the mineral sphalerite and industrial form
  • Lead sulfide (PbS) — the mineral galena
  • Calcium sulfide (CaS) — one calcium ion per sulfide ion

Sulfide Ion vs Other Sulfur Anions

The sulfide ion S²¯ is not the only sulfur anion. The most common sulfur anions and their compound classes:

  • S²¯ — sulfide ion (this page)
  • HS¹¯ — bisulfide/hydrosulfide ion. See HS¹¯ Ion.
  • S&sub2;²¯ — persulfide or disulfide ion (in ionic disulfides like pyrite). See Disulfide.
  • SO&sub3;²¯ — sulfite ion (unrelated to sulfide). See Sulfite.
  • HSO&sub3;¹¯ — bisulfite ion (unrelated to bisulfide). See Bisulfite.
  • SO&sub4;²¯ — sulfate ion (unrelated to sulfide). See Sulfat.
