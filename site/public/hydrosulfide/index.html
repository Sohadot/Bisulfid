Compound Class · HS⁻ · Alternative Name for Bisulfide

Hydrosulfide

Hydrosulfide is an alternative name for the bisulfide compound class. Both names refer to compounds of the HS¹¯ ion. German: Hydrosulfid. The choice between bisulfide and hydrosulfide varies by context; both describe the same ion and the same compound class.

Same Ion, Two Names

The HS¹¯ ion goes by several names:

  • Bisulfide ion — most common in industrial and commercial contexts
  • Hydrosulfide ion — common in academic chemistry
  • Hydrogen sulfide ion — systematic name based on H&sub2;S
  • Sulfanide ion — formal IUPAC-adjacent name

A compound called sodium hydrosulfide and one called sodium bisulfide are the same compound (NaHS).

Hydrosulfid in German

In German, the corresponding name is Hydrosulfid — following the -id suffix convention. The compound can be called Natriumbisulfid or Natriumhydrosulfid — both refer to NaHS.

Hydrosulfide vs Sulfide

Hydrosulfide (HS¹¯) and sulfide (S²¯) are different. Hydrosulfide retains one hydrogen with a single negative charge. Sulfide has no hydrogen and carries a double negative charge. See the disambiguation page for the three-way comparison.
