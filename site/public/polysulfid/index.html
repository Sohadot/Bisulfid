Polysulfid

Polysulfid is the German-form name for what English calls polysulfide. The final E is dropped: polysulfide (EN) becomes Polysulfid (DE). This follows the same -id/-ide suffix pattern as Bisulfid/bisulfide, Sulfid/sulfide, and Disulfid/disulfide.

What Polysulfid Names

Polysulfid (polysulfide) compounds contain chains of sulfur atoms as the anion. The general formula is S⊂n²¯ where n is 3 or more. Common examples include sodium polysulfide (Natriumpolysulfid, Na&sub2;S⊂n;) and potassium polysulfide (Kaliumpolysulfid, K&sub2;S⊂n;).

Polysulfid in the Sulfur Chain Family

Polysulfid extends the sulfur chain compound series:

  • Sulfid — simple S²¯ (n=1 effectively)
  • Disulfid — S–S bond (n=2)
  • Polysulfid — S⊂n²¯ (n≥3)
