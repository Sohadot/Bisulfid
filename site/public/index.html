Atlas Thesis

The German chemical naming system drops the final E from many compound names: bisulfid instead of bisulfide, sulfid instead of sulfide. This is not an error. It is a systematic convention with industrial, multilingual, and ontological consequences.

bisulfid.com holds the German-form spelling deliberately. The domain is the spelling boundary. The atlas it supports maps the full compound family — bisulfide, sulfide, hydrosulfide, disulfide, molybdenum disulfide — across both naming conventions and industrial contexts.

This is not a bisulfite sequencing site. Bisulfite (HSO₃¹¯) is a distinct compound class. It appears in this atlas only as a disambiguation node — to clarify what bisulfid.com is not, and to name the boundary precisely.
