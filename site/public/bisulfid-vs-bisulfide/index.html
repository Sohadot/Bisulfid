Nomenclature · Spelling Boundary

Bisulfid vs Bisulfide: The Missing Letter

The domain is bisulfid.com — not bisulfide.com. The difference is a single letter: the final E. That letter is not missing. It belongs to the English form.

Two Spellings, One Compound

Bisulfide and Bisulfid refer to the same underlying compound class. The difference in spelling reflects a systematic pattern in how German and English chemical nomenclature handle the suffix for this compound class.

  • English form: bisulfide
  • German form: bisulfid

This is not an error in the domain name. It is the point of the domain name.

The E Was Not Dropped

A common misreading of bisulfid.com is that the domain has a typo — that the E was accidentally left off.

The E was not dropped. The E belongs to the English spelling. The German spelling does not use it. The domain chooses the German form deliberately. That choice is the conceptual architecture of the asset.

The Spelling Boundary as Architecture

The boundary between bisulfide and bisulfid — between the English form and the German form — is where this asset positions itself.

bisulfid.com occupies the German-form spelling of a compound with chemical and industrial relevance. This positioning is not accidental and not correctable. The German identity layer of this asset carries particular significance precisely because the domain name derives from German nomenclature conventions.

What This Is Not

  • This is not a claim that the German spelling is chemically superior.
  • This is not a claim that English speakers use the wrong term.
  • This is not a nomenclature dispute.

It is an architectural statement: the asset exists at the boundary, and the boundary is real.

The Broader Pattern

The German/English suffix difference extends across the sulfur compound family. Sulfid vs sulfide, bisulfid vs bisulfide, chlorid vs chloride, oxid vs oxide — German chemical nomenclature systematically uses the shorter form without the trailing E. bisulfid.com maps this pattern as part of its multilingual architecture.
