German -id Form · Phosphide · Pnictide Family

Phosphid

Phosphid is the German-form name for what English calls phosphide. The final E is dropped: phosphide (EN) becomes Phosphid (DE). Phosphid belongs to the pnictide group alongside Nitrid (nitride) in the German -id compound class naming convention.

What Phosphid Names

Phosphid (phosphide) compounds contain phosphorus in a negative oxidation state, most commonly as P³¯. Examples include iron phosphide (Eisenphosphid, Fe&sub2;P), indium phosphide (Indiumphosphid, InP — a semiconductor material), and gallium phosphide (Galliumphosphid, GaP).

Phosphid in the -id Pattern

Phosphid illustrates how the German -id suffix covers pnictide compounds (Group 15 elements as anions). The pnictide group in German -id form: Nitrid (N³¯) and Phosphid (P³¯). Both follow the same -id convention as the chalcogenides and halides. See German Chemical Suffix.
