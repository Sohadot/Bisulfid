Terminology

This page indexes the sulfur compound terminology covered in the bisulfid.com atlas. Each term has its own reference page with compound class context, formula, and German/English naming information. The atlas focuses on the naming and classification of sulfur compounds — not procurement, safety, or industrial performance.

Core HS⁻ Terms

These terms name the bisulfide/hydrosulfide ion (HS¹¯) and its salts:

  • Bisulfide — English compound class name for HS¹¯ compounds
  • Hydrosulfide — alternative English name for the same HS¹¯ class
  • Bisulfid — German form; the domain identity term for bisulfid.com
  • HS⁻ Ion — the bisulfide/hydrosulfide anion
  • Sodium Bisulfide — NaHS, the reference specific compound

Sulfide Class Terms

  • Sulfide — S²¯ compound class
  • Sulfide Ion — the S²¯ anion
  • Sulfid — German form of sulfide
  • Sodium Sulfide — Na&sub2;S
  • Iron Sulfide — FeS and FeS&sub2; compounds
  • Zinc Sulfide — ZnS

Disulfide and Related Terms

  • Disulfide — S–S bond compound class
  • Disulfid — German form of disulfide
  • Disulfide Bonds — S–S bond chemistry
  • Polysulfide — S⁻⁻ chain compounds
  • Polysulfid — German form of polysulfide
  • Pyrite — FeS&sub2;, the natural iron disulfide mineral

Oxysulfur Terms

  • Bisulfite — HSO&sub3;¹¯ compounds; NOT bisulfide
  • Sulfite — SO&sub3;²¯ compounds
  • Sulfat — German form of sulfate (SO&sub4;²¯)
  • Thiosulfate — S&sub2;O&sub3;²¯ compounds
  • Sulfuric Acid — H&sub2;SO&sub4;

Organic Sulfur Terms

  • Thiol — R–SH organic compounds
  • Hydrogen Sulfide — H&sub2;S, the parent acid
