# Corpus methodology (non-public draft)

**Draft status:** This file is a **non-public draft**. It is **not published**, **not indexable**, **not in the sitemap**, **not approved for public use**, and **not publication-ready**. It requires **draft review**, **source-locking** where factual assertions apply, and editorial signoff before any public tier.

Bisulfid.com is being built as **reference infrastructure** for a **sovereign chemical-language corpus**—not as a generic chemistry blog. This page describes **how that corpus is governed** before anything is treated as publicly authoritative.

## Why the corpus is governed before publication

Publication without governance would recycle two failure modes the project rejects: **weak visibility** (thin pages that rank without earning trust) and **generic chemistry content** (undifferentiated explainers that do not defend German–English chemical language discipline). [SOURCE REQUIRED] Candidate doctrinal and planning documents describe minimum gates; this draft does not claim those records are fully source-locked for public citation.

**Route planning**, **draft creation**, **source-locking**, **claim approval**, and **public launch** are **separate steps**. Planning a `route_id` in `routes.json` does not create a public page. A Markdown draft does not satisfy the Quality Gate. Sources must map to a governed registry before factual claims harden. Claim registries remain **inactive** in the current repository posture; **no claim is approved** by this draft. Public launch remains **blocked** until route, source, claim, and linking conditions are met.

## Minimum launch scale versus long-term ambition

The **300-page** figure is widely framed as a **minimum launch cohort** threshold for a **sovereign reference** layer—not the final size of the corpus. [SOURCE REQUIRED] Exact threshold language should be verified against current doctrine and corpus planning files during source-locking. The intent is **depth and repeatability**, not a one-time rush to fill slots with weak pages.

## Corpus roles, boundaries, and linking roles

Each page in the planned corpus is expected to carry:

- A **corpus role** (why the page exists in the system, not merely what topic it mentions).
- **Source boundaries** (what may be asserted only with registry-backed support).
- **Claim boundaries** (which claim families apply; no silent expansion into market, safety, procurement, or operations advice).
- **Internal linking roles** (how the page strengthens hubs, spines, and disambiguation—see `internal_linking_discipline`).

Illustrative relationships (plain `route_id` references only—no assumption these routes are public):

- `glossary` — ontology-driven term chamber; density expectations apply.
- `sulfur_compounds` — gateway bridge into compound-family language.
- `bisulfide_hydrosulfide_sulfide` — disambiguation authority for naming precision.
- `german_english_chemical_terms` — identity-layer crosswalk infrastructure.
- `sources` — public bibliography face when the source registry supports it.
- `internal_linking_discipline` — editorial rules for route_id-centric linking.
- `quality_gate_public_explainer` — publication gate explanation; draft review required.

This methodology draft **does not** recommend publication now, **does not** imply all planned routes are ready, and **does not** imply any claim is approved.
