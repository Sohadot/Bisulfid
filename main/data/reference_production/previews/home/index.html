BISULFID

A reference project on the language of sulfur chemistry: German and English terms, the compounds they name, and the evidence behind each statement.

Start with a governed reference

The first reference record built under the current rules covers molybdenum disulfide (MoS2): its formula, its layered bulk structure, the 1T, 2H and 3R polytypes, and two independent determinations of the 2H lattice parameter. It rests on 11 admitted evidence records, each traced to the study or database that produced it.

The question in our name

'Bisulfid' and 'bisulfide' differ by one letter. Whether 'Bisulfid' is simply the German spelling of the English term is itself a terminology claim, and it is handled like any other claim on this site: it will be stated as fact only after its sources are verified and admitted. 4 related terminology claims are registered and still under review.

  • Bisulfid and bisulfide: the terminology record

How a statement reaches a reference page

A source is first identified and checked, then qualified for a specific use. A passage from it is recorded as evidence with its exact location, and a claim is approved only against admitted evidence. Numbers are quoted as their sources print them; when two determinations differ, both are shown rather than averaged. A page is published only if it adds knowledge that no other page on the site already holds.
