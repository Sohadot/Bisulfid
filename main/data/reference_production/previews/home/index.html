The question in our name

'Bisulfid' and 'bisulfide' differ by one letter. Whether 'Bisulfid' is simply the German spelling of the English term is itself a terminology claim, and it is handled like any other claim on this site: it will be stated as fact only after its sources are verified and admitted.

The terminology record sets out, from 15 admitted evidence records and 11 reviewed claims, which meaning dictionaries, IUPAC and a chemical database attach to each spelling, and where the evidence stops. 2 related claims remain open.

How a statement reaches a reference page

A source is first identified and checked, then qualified for a specific use. A passage from it is recorded as evidence with its exact location, and a claim is approved only against admitted evidence. Numbers are quoted as their sources print them; when two determinations differ, both are shown rather than averaged. A new reference page is published only if it adds knowledge that no other page on the site already holds; most older pages were published before these rules and are being reviewed or rebuilt.

Who is responsible for this site

BISULFID is an independent reference project published by the owner of the bisulfid.com domain. It is not affiliated with IUPAC, with any dictionary publisher or with any company or database named on its pages.

Each reference page built under these rules names the evidence behind every statement and shows when it was retrieved. No individual expert review is claimed: a page records that it has been independently validated only after such a review has taken place.
