# Corpus Production Multilingual Model — Layer 2

**Sprint:** 5O-A  
**Date:** 2026-05-29

---

## Purpose

Define multilingual production discipline for English, German, Arabic, Chinese, and Japanese layers — controlled terminology records, not translation spam.

---

## English base layer

- Primary bisulfide/sulfur terminology reference language for international retrieval
- Element and compound records governed under EN chemical authority tiers where applicable
- English pages are **controlled terminology records**, not simple translations of DE content

---

## German identity/lexical layer

- DE core terminology and lexical identity layer for German chemical document language
- German lexical authority required for DE surface forms
- DE pages remain non-public drafts until source/claim gates pass

---

## Arabic strategic accessibility layer

- Strategic accessibility layer for Arabic-language sulfur terminology access
- **Deferred** until source governance, hreflang, and terminology rules ready for ar layer
- No low-quality translation spam

---

## Chinese industrial/technical layer

- Industrial and technical document-language layer for Chinese contexts
- **Deferred** until source, hreflang, and terminology governance ready
- Controlled terminology role per page — not bulk translation

---

## Japanese advanced technical language layer

- Advanced technical language layer for Japanese industrial/chemical contexts
- **Deferred** until source, hreflang, and terminology governance ready
- Anti-translation-spam rules apply

---

## Why multilingual pages are controlled records, not simple translations

- Each language page has distinct **authority posture** and **terminology role**
- Multilingual pages are **not simple translations** — they are governed records
- Equivalence claims require verified sources per language
- Universal suffix or cross-language identity claims forbidden without formal review

---

## hreflang readiness

- hreflang groups must be coherent before multilingual publication
- Planning-stage `route_id` references only — no live hreflang in public output pre-gate
- hreflang readiness gate precedes ar/zh/ja public expansion

---

## Translation registry readiness

- `translation_registry.json` planning precedes multilingual publication waves
- Translation status tracked per route (`source_planned`, etc.)
- No publication without registry alignment

---

## Source governance per language

- EN scientific database authority ≠ DE lexical dictionary authority
- Each language layer requires applicable source categories per `SOURCE_POLICY.md`
- Teaching references supporting context only — not sole formal authority

---

## Anti-translation-spam rules

- **No low-quality translation spam**
- **No bulk translation allowed** without terminology governance charter
- Machine translation output is not a source
- Each multilingual page must have a **controlled terminology role**
- ar/zh/ja expansion remains **deferred** until governance ready

---

## Multilingual expansion gates

| Gate | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Terminology role | Documented per page |
| Source posture | Language-appropriate authority |
| hreflang | Group coherent |
| Translation registry | Row planned |
| Draft discipline | non_public, markers preserved |
| Publication | Deferred for ar/zh/ja until all above pass |

**Multilingual publication without hreflang planning is forbidden.**
