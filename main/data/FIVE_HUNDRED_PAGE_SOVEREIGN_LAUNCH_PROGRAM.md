# Five Hundred Page Sovereign Launch Program (Sprint 5H)

## Why Sprint 5H exists

Sprint **5G** increased the **real non-public draft corpus** (18 of 26 registered routes now have bodies), proving controlled production works—but the repository remains **far below** the authority threshold required for a credible first public launch.

Sprint **5A** established a **300-page minimum** launch cohort. The owner has now judged that standard **too low** for Bisulfid.com’s intended position as a **sovereign-grade, multilingual, academically disciplined chemical-language reference asset**.

Sprint **5H** is a **strategic architecture correction and production-program sprint**. It upgrades the public launch threshold to **500 governed pages**, defines how to reach that threshold, and establishes wave-based production discipline—**without** creating content, publishing routes, or modifying registries.

---

## Why 300 pages is no longer enough

A **300-page** first surface risks:

- **Thin authority signal** — professional users (chemists, translators, compliance teams) expect depth comparable to serious reference systems, not a large blog.
- **Weak multilingual credibility** — EN+DE+AR+ZH+JA layers cannot reach meaningful density at 300 without sacrificing quality or skipping entire language communities.
- **SEO without substance** — search architecture needs **cluster depth** (terminology spine, disambiguation, governance, index maps) that 300 pages cannot fully support without placeholder behavior.
- **Internal-link fragility** — hubs and disambiguation nodes require enough spokes to avoid **broken or aspirational graphs** at launch.

**300 pages was a planning floor; it is not sufficient for the owner’s sovereign-grade standard.**

---

## Why 500 pages is the minimum launch threshold

**500 governed reference pages** is the new **minimum first public launch cohort** because it allows:

1. **English global base layer** with gateway, terminology spine, and disambiguation depth.
2. **German–English terminology layer** with identity and lexical boundary coverage.
3. **German authority layer** as editorially independent records—not MT mirrors.
4. **Arabic, Chinese, and Japanese technical reference layers** at **starter authority depth** (not full 3,000-page multilingual maturity, but non-token presence).
5. **Governance and methodology pages** that make source/claim discipline legible.
6. **Index/map infrastructure** without thin link farms.
7. **Industrial/document-language pages** at vocabulary scope—without market-report drift.
8. **Strong internal-linking clusters** with enough nodes to validate graph integrity before launch.

**500 is achievable under wave production** (`CORPUS_PRODUCTION_WAVE_MODEL.md`) while preserving gates—unlike an ungoverned rush to 3,000.

## Strategic thesis for a 500-page launch

Bisulfid.com’s first public surface must demonstrate:

- **Scientific discipline** — sourced, bounded claims; no generic AI glossary tone.
- **Multilingual authority** — controlled terminology records per language, not translation spam.
- **Source governance** — registry-backed public copy; teaching sources as candidate-only where policy requires.
- **Claim governance** — approved claims only where registries are active and scoped.
- **Strong internal linking** — route_id-centric graph; **no broken links** at launch.
- **Strong SEO architecture** — metadata aligned to real corpus roles; no thin SEO gates.
- **Technical/security integrity** — validated build and indexation posture before output ships.
- **No placeholder pages, no thin pages, no fake publication readiness.**

Launch is **authority-first**, not traffic-first.
