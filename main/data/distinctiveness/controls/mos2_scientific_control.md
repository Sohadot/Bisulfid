# Molybdenum Disulfide (MoS2) — Scientific Identity and Bulk Structure

## Chemical identity

The formula of molybdenum disulfide is MoS2, as recorded independently by the PubChem and NIST chemistry databases. This is a statement of composition only: it does not describe how the atoms are arranged in the solid, and the word "molecular" in database field names should not be read as implying a discrete molecular solid.

## Bulk structure: stacked S–Mo–S layers

Bulk MoS2 is a layered material in which each layer is a three-atom-thick S–Mo–S stack. The layered description applies to the bulk crystal; properties of single layers or few-layer flakes are a separate question that this reference does not answer.

## The 2H polytype

In the 2H polytype, bulk MoS2 adopts the hexagonal space group P6₃/mmc (No. 194), with a c-axis repeat near 12.3 Å. This identification rests on two crystallographic determinations with distinct origins: Dickinson and Pauling's 1923 X-ray study of molybdenite and the 1983 determination by Schönfeld, Huang and Moss. Dickinson and Pauling did not use the modern label "2H"; the explicit 2H/3R terminology appears in the 1983 study, whose title refers to 2H- and 3R-MoS2.

## Lattice parameter a: two coexisting determinations

Two independent determinations report the in-plane lattice parameter a of bulk 2H-MoS2 as 3.15(2) Å (Dickinson and Pauling, 1923) and 3.161 Å (Schönfeld, Huang and Moss, 1983). The two values agree within the stated uncertainty and are kept as separate determinations rather than averaged into a single canonical number.

| --- | --- | --- |
| Dickinson & Pauling (1923) | 3.15(2) Å | bulk 2H |
| Schönfeld, Huang & Moss (1983) | 3.161 Å | bulk 2H |

## 2H is not 3R

The 3R polytype of MoS2 is a distinct structure with rhombohedral space group R3m and approximate lattice parameters a ≈ 3.17 Å and c ≈ 18.38 Å, so its values must not be attributed to the 2H form. A lattice value quoted without its polytype is therefore incomplete.
